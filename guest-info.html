Elegant Andalusian finca

Spain Wedding

Our Spain celebration will take place at Finca Mesa Jardín, a traditional Andalusian cortijo surrounded by gardens, olive trees, almond trees and countryside views towards the Sierra de Grazalema.

Typical October day24–26°C
Typical night14–17°C
Guest moodSpanish finca

The setting

Expect warm southern Spanish light, garden drinks, cortijo textures and a countryside atmosphere. This is not a coastal wedding; it is a finca celebration in the hills near Arcos de la Frontera.

Dress comfortably for outdoor spaces, garden paths and a relaxed evening in the countryside.
Cortijo characterTraditional Andalusian finca architecture and patio atmosphere.
Garden spacesOutdoor areas surrounded by greenery and rural views.
Sierra viewsOpen countryside looking towards Grazalema.
Nearby ArcosA beautiful white village close to the venue.

What to wear

👗
Wedding outfitSmart finca wedding style — breathable dresses, linen blends, shirts, lightweight suits and polished but comfortable shoes.
🧥
Evening layerBring a wrap, blazer or cardigan for after sunset, when the countryside air may feel cooler.
👞
ShoesBlock heels, loafers, smart boots or flats are best for garden paths and patio areas.

Where it is

Finca Mesa Jardín is near Arcos de la Frontera, around 35 minutes from Jerez de la Frontera by motorway.

Nearest practical airport: JerezAlso consider: SevilleNearby: Arcos de la FronteraSetting: Sierra de Grazalema views
South Africa19 December 2026Mission House · KZN Midlands
Mission House · KZN Midlands

South Africa Wedding

Our South African celebration will take place at Mission House in the KwaZulu-Natal Midlands — a green countryside setting with lawns, open fields, soft summer light and a relaxed country-estate feel.

Typical December day25–26°C
Typical night14–15°C
Guest moodMidlands country estate

The setting

Expect a warm summer wedding in the Midlands countryside: grass underfoot, outdoor movement between spaces, green views and an easy, welcoming atmosphere.

The feel is polished but relaxed — a countryside celebration rather than a formal city event.
Green surroundingsLawns, fields and natural Midlands scenery.
Country-estate feelWarm, relaxed and elegant without feeling stiff.
Summer weatherPrepare for sunshine and the chance of an afternoon shower.
Comfort mattersChoose shoes and layers that work for lawns and outdoor spaces.

What the weather should feel like

December in the KZN Midlands is usually warm and green, with a real chance of afternoon or evening rain.

Pack for summer, but bring a light rain-friendly layer just in case.

What to wear

👒
Wedding outfitSmart countryside wedding style: summer dresses, linen, lightweight suits, block heels, loafers or smart flats.
🌧️
Rain planBring a light layer for transfers between accommodation and venue spaces.
👞
ShoesChoose shoes that are comfortable on lawns. Avoid narrow stilettos on grass.

Where it is

Mission House is in the KwaZulu-Natal Midlands near Howick, close to the Midlands Meander countryside routes.

Nearest major airport: Durban / King ShakaNearby towns: Howick, Hilton, Nottingham RoadTravel corridor: Durban / Howick / Midlands
