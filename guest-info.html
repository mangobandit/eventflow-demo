SpainSaturday 10 October 2026Cádiz / Chiclana area

Spain wedding guest guide

Expect a relaxed Andalusian coastal wedding feel: warm days, cooler evenings, beach-town energy and plenty nearby for guests staying around Chiclana, La Barrosa, Sancti Petri, Cádiz or Jerez.

Typical October day24-26°C
Typical night14-17°C
Rain riskRising

Weather expectation

October is usually pleasantly warm in the Chiclana/Jerez area. Historical averages point to warm afternoons and mild-to-cool evenings, with rain becoming more likely as the month progresses. Guests should plan for sunshine, a sea breeze and a possible light shower.

Pack layers: daytime linen/cotton, sunglasses and sun cream, plus a light jacket or wrap for the evening.

What to wear

👗
Wedding outfitSummer wedding smart, but not beachwear. Think breathable dresses, suits in lighter fabrics, shirts, linen blends and comfortable shoes.
🧥
Evening layerTemperatures drop after sunset, especially near the coast. Bring a blazer, shawl, cardigan or light jacket.
👞
ShoesAvoid very delicate heels if walking on cobbles, gravel, grass or beach-adjacent paths. Block heels, loafers and smart flats are safer.

Where it is

Guest logistics in the planner point to Chiclana/Jerez transport groups, with the wider wedding area shown as Cádiz, Spain. Useful bases for guests are Chiclana de la Frontera, La Barrosa, Novo Sancti Petri, Cádiz city and Jerez de la Frontera.

Nearest practical airport: JerezAlso consider: SevilleBeach base: La Barrosa / Sancti PetriCity break: Cádiz

Things to do nearby

🏖️
La Barrosa beachA wide golden-sand beach, sunset walks, beach restaurants and an easy holiday base.
🏰
Sancti PetriCastle views, marina atmosphere, boat trips, kayaking and coastal activities.
🍷
Jerez day tripSherry bodegas, flamenco culture, old-town lunches and an easy airport connection.
🌊
Cádiz cityOld-town wandering, tapas, beaches, sea walls and a classic Andalusian coastal day out.
South AfricaSaturday 19 December 2026Mission House · KZN Midlands

South Africa wedding guest guide

Expect a green Midlands summer wedding: warm days, long daylight, dramatic views, relaxed countryside stays and a real chance of afternoon or evening rain.

Typical December day25-26°C
Typical night14-15°C
Rain riskHigh

Weather expectation

December in Howick / Curry's Post is summer: warm, green and often wet. Historical averages show comfortable daytime temperatures, cooler evenings and frequent rain days, so guests should be prepared for sunshine and a passing storm.

Pack for summer, but bring a rain-friendly layer and shoes that can handle damp grass.

What to wear

🤠
Wedding outfitSmart countryside wedding style. Breathable fabrics, dresses, shirts, chinos, summer suits and cowboy/western touches all work.
🌧️
Rain planBring a light rain jacket or wrap. Umbrellas can help for accommodation transfers, but check venue flow on the day.
🥾
ShoesChoose wedges, block heels, boots, loafers or smart flats. Avoid stilettos if moving over lawns or farm-style ground.

Where it is

Mission House is in the KwaZulu-Natal Midlands on Curry's Post Road outside Howick. The venue describes itself as a relaxed Midlands accommodation and event space close to the Midlands Meander's attractions.

Nearest major airport: Durban / King ShakaNearby towns: Howick, Hilton, Nottingham RoadHoliday style: countryside, food, views, craft routeTransport groups: Durban / Howick
