Where to stay

Base yourself for calm arrivals.

Nearby accommodation options are listed below. Wedding-day shuttles are confirmed for both celebrations: Chiclana for Spain, and Durban via Howick for South Africa.

Spain

Arcos, Jerez or Chiclana

Arcos de la Frontera (about 15 minutes away) is best for countryside atmosphere and a short wedding-day journey. Jerez (30–40 minutes) offers stronger transport links, restaurants and a larger hotel choice. Chiclana (about 60 minutes) has plenty of accommodation near the beach, with a wedding-day shuttle to the venue and back. All options are on Booking.com and Airbnb.

  • Best for the venue: Arcos (15 min)
  • Best for flights and nightlife: Jerez (30–40 min)
  • Beach base with wedding shuttle: Chiclana (60 min)
South Africa

Howick, the Midlands or Durban

Howick (about 20 minutes away) is the closest town with accommodation. The venue sits in the Midlands Meander, with plenty of boutique hotels and guest houses nearby. Durban (1 hr 20 min) also works well — we recommend uMhlanga and Ballito — with a wedding-day shuttle to the venue and back. All options are on Booking.com and Airbnb.

  • Closest town: Howick (20 min)
  • Best for experience: Midlands Meander / Curry’s Post
  • Coastal base with wedding shuttle: Durban, uMhlanga or Ballito

Frequently asked

Before you message us…

We will keep expanding this section as plans are confirmed.

What is the wedding theme?

Rodeo-style wedding celebrations built around Western music and great BBQ food. Cowboy boots, hats, leather and denim are welcome, and encouraged. Don't have a cowboy hat? We'll provide one for you.

What can I expect on the day?

Food, drinks, laughs and a relaxed Rodeo-style celebration. Depending on which wedding you are attending, there will be small Western-inspired games and entertainment, such as horseshoe toss and other fun touches to keep the day moving.

What kind of food will there be?

Expect a Western-inspired feast: BBQ and braai-style meats, with much of the food cooked over open fire. There will be options for lighter grazers as well as those who want something more hearty.

What should we wear?

The dress feel is Rodeo Western. Cowboy boots, hats, leather, denim, belts, bolo ties, fringe, country shirts and country-style dresses are welcome and encouraged. Choose shoes that work on gardens, lawns and a dancefloor.

Is everything in the same location?

Yes. The ceremony, food, drinks and celebration are all planned around the same venue, so once you arrive you can settle in and enjoy the day without moving between locations.

Is the wedding indoors or outdoors?

The wedding has a country/Rodeo feel and is planned as an outdoor celebration. If the weather turns, there will be cover under a tent or suitable shelter so the day can keep flowing comfortably.

How early can I arrive?

You may arrive up to two hours before the official start time if you need to. Please note that the bar will remain closed until the official kick-off time, so arriving early is mainly for settling in and avoiding a rush.

What are the timings for the day?

We will keep the day running with clear start and stop times, so please arrive promptly and avoid being late. If you want a drink before the ceremony, please grab it before the bar closes for the ceremony start. The full run sheets for both weddings are published in the celebration sections above.

Will there be wedding-day transport?

Yes, shuttle buses run for both weddings. Spain: Chiclana to the venue departing 16:45, returning from the venue at midnight; there is also venue parking and taxis to Arcos and Jerez (book in advance). South Africa: Durban (via Howick) to the venue departing 15:30, returning at 23:00, with parking available at the venue too.

Is there parking at the venue?

Yes, there will be parking available at the venue. We will share any final parking or arrival notes closer to the wedding date.

Can children come?

Children are very welcome. We will provide things to help keep them entertained, and there will be people nearby to keep a friendly eye on them, but parents and guardians remain responsible for their children throughout the celebration.

What gifts should I bring?

No need for gifts! Just grab your boots and join us. Your presence is the greatest gift we could ask for.

Can I take photos or post online?

You are welcome to take a few personal photos, but please do not post the day publicly online without our permission. This is a private, intimate celebration and we have invested in a professional photography team. We will also use a private social wall, Walls.io, so guests can share moments in a more controlled and private way.

What is Walls.io?

Walls.io is a private social media wall for the wedding. Instead of everyone posting publicly, guests can share selected photos and messages into one private wedding feed. It lets us collect the fun moments while keeping the day more intimate and controlled. We will share the details closer to the time.

When should we book flights?

Once your attendance is confirmed, begin tracking routes and book when the itinerary and price feel right. Prefer flexible fares where possible because regional routes and timings can change.
