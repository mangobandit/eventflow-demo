⚒ Product angle

Upload your wedding docs. Get a live run sheet, budget tracker, vendor dashboard and guest logistics board in minutes.

EventFlow is less of a normal wedding checklist app and more like a document-to-dashboard planning system for destination weddings.

⚒ MVP hook

1. Document-first onboarding

Start with supplier contracts, PDFs, RSVP sheets and spreadsheets.

2. AI extraction

Pull out costs, dates, rules, tasks, dietaries, transport needs and deadlines.

3. Planner command centre

Turn messy wedding admin into one shared operating dashboard.
