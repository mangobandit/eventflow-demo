Travel concierge

Choose the journey that suits your trip.

Book flexible travel where possible. Regional routes can be seasonal, so compare the nearest airport with the wider route alternatives before committing.

ES

Getting to Spain

Andalusia, made simple.

XRY

Jerez Airport

Closest practical airport for Arcos. Best when a suitable direct or connecting route is available.

Closest option
SVQ

Seville Airport

Usually the strongest balance of flight choice and driving time for European guests.

Recommended default
AGP / GIB

Málaga or Gibraltar Airport

Málaga usually offers the broadest international choice, while Gibraltar can be a useful alternative for some UK routes or onward plans.

Most flight choice
3 nights

Suggested wedding weekend route

  1. Thursday: arrive via Jerez or Seville; settle in Arcos or Jerez.
  2. Friday: old town lunch, finca country drive and an early evening.
  3. Saturday: wedding day at Finca Mesa Jardín.
  4. Sunday: relaxed brunch before departure.
7 nights

Suggested Andalusian celebration

  1. 2 nights Seville: architecture, tapas and evening walks.
  2. 2 nights Cádiz or Chiclana: beach, seafood and Atlantic sunsets.
  3. 3 nights Arcos/Jerez: white villages, bodegas and the wedding.
  4. Optional detour: Grazalema, Zahara or Ronda by car.
Our practical recommendation: Seville is the safest all round starting point; Jerez is the closest; Málaga/Gibraltar is best for guests prioritising route choice, UK links or a longer holiday.
ZA

Getting to South Africa

Durban, Midlands, then adventure.

DUR

King Shaka International

The main arrival airport for the KZN Midlands, with the broadest rental car and transfer options.

Recommended default
PZB

Pietermaritzburg

Closer to Howick and the Midlands, but with a smaller domestic schedule and fewer backup options.

Closest regional
JNB

Johannesburg connection

A common international gateway. Connect onward to Durban rather than driving after a long haul flight.

International gateway
4 nights

Suggested wedding weekend route

  1. Thursday: fly to Durban and transfer to the Midlands.
  2. Friday: Midlands Meander lunch and gentle exploring.
  3. Saturday: wedding day at Mission House.
  4. Sunday: brunch, Howick stop and onward travel.
10 nights

Suggested KZN celebration

  1. 2 nights Durban: coast, food and an easy long haul landing.
  2. 4 nights Midlands: wedding, Meander and country stays.
  3. 2 nights Drakensberg: mountain scenery and restorative time.
  4. 2+ nights: add a safari reserve or continue to Cape Town.
Our practical recommendation: fly into Durban, book a transfer or rental car ahead of time, and avoid a late night self drive to the Midlands after long haul travel.

Where to stay

Base yourself for calm arrivals.

Nearby accommodation options are listed below. Transport pickup points will be published once supplier routes are locked.

Spain

Arcos or Jerez

Arcos de la Frontera is best for countryside atmosphere and a short wedding day journey. Jerez offers stronger transport links, restaurants and a larger hotel choice.

  • Best for the venue: Arcos
  • Best for flights and nightlife: Jerez
  • Beach extension: Cádiz, Chiclana or Vejer
South Africa

Howick or the Midlands Meander

Howick is practical for shops and local transfers. Midlands country stays offer the most memorable wedding weekend atmosphere.

  • Best for logistics: Howick
  • Best for experience: Curry’s Post / Nottingham Road area
  • Coastal extension: Umhlanga or Durban

Frequently asked

Before you message us...

We will keep expanding this section as plans are confirmed.

When should we book flights?

Once your attendance is confirmed, begin tracking routes and book when the itinerary and price feel right. Prefer flexible fares because regional schedules can change.

Will there be wedding day transport?

We are planning route groups for Spain and South Africa. Final pickup locations depend on where guests stay, so please answer guest check in transport questions promptly when they are sent.

Can children come?

Children are very welcome. We will provide things to help keep them entertained, and parents and guardians remain responsible for their children throughout the celebration.

What should we wear?

The dress feel is Rodeo Western. Cowboy boots, hats, leather, denim, belts, bolo ties, fringe, country shirts and country style dresses are welcome and encouraged. Choose shoes that work on gardens, lawns and a dancefloor.

When will final timings be shared?

The key guest timings are shown above. Exact pickup points, arrival notes and weather guidance will be refreshed closer to each wedding week.
